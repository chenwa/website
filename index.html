Intro

Hi, I'm Warren

In the 2000's I watched my dad code during the early stages of the internet, the dot com boom. I was a child then, who was obsessed with inventing things. I built a light that would turn off when my door opened so my mom woudldn't catch me reading late at night, and a cereal cup that kept the milk and cereal seperate but could be drunk on the go. I saw the internet as a new frontier for invention and decided very early on that I wanted to be a part of it. I went on to study computer science and mathematics at the University of California, San Diego, where I was also a part of their NCAA Division I fencing team. Since then I've built several project and contributed to enterprise level code bases. Explore my work experience and projects.
